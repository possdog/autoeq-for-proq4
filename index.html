AutoEq.app for Pro-Q 4

Instructions

  1. Go to autoeq.app and select your headphones using the search bar at the top.
    1. Use the Profiles section on the left to customize your desired target curve.
    2. On the right-hand section, select EqualizerAPO ParametricEq as your equalizer app and enter your desired sample rate.
    3. Click the download button at the bottom of the right-hand section. You should receive a file ending with ParametricEq.txt.
  2. Click the Choose Files button below to select your EQ file(s).
  3. Click the Convert button below. Your Pro-Q 4 preset files should download automatically.
  4. Load your new preset(s) in Pro-Q 4.
    1. Locate your Pro-Q 4 presets folder. On Windows, this is Documents\FabFilter\Pro-Q 4.
    2. If you have more than one set of headphones and/or want more than one target curve option, consider creating a new subfolder (e.g. Headphones) to stay organized.
    3. Move the presets (.ffp files) to this folder. You may need to reload your instance(s) of Pro-Q 4 for your new presets to appear.
  5. Enjoy the new, corrected frequency response of your headphones!
